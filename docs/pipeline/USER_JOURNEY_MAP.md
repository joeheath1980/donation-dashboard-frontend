# User (Donor) Journey Map - Do-Nation Platform

## Journey Overview
The donor journey on Do-Nation begins with discovering their lifetime giving score through email scraping, then branches into two pathways: periodic direct charity donations and frequent micro matched donations with businesses.

## 🎯 User Goals
- Discover lifetime giving score and impact
- Find new charities and projects to support
- Maximize impact through business micro matching
- Build sustainable giving habits through small, frequent donations

---

## 📍 Journey Stages

### 1. **Discovery & Score Revelation**
**Entry Points:**
- "Find out your giving score" campaigns
- Search friend's public profiles
- Browse top givers leaderboard
- Charity/business profile discovery

**The Magic Moment:**
1. Sign up with email/social login
2. Connect email for receipt scanning
3. **AI scans lifetime of donation receipts**
4. Watch score calculate in real-time
5. **Reveal: "You've donated $3,847 to 12 charities!"**
6. Receive tier status and badges
7. **Create public profile** (optional)

**Public Profile Setup:**
- Choose privacy level (full public/friends/private)
- Display score, tier, badges
- Show favorite charities
- Share giving philosophy
- Add profile photo
- Generate shareable link

**Emotions:** 
- 😲 Surprised by lifetime total
- 🏆 Proud to share achievements
- 💡 Inspired by other profiles
- 🎯 Motivated to climb leaderboard

**Key Insights Revealed:**
- Total lifetime donations (money)
- Volunteer hours tracked
- Fundraising campaigns completed
- Number of charities supported
- Ranking among peers
- Initial tier and badges earned

---

### 2. **Two Donation Pathways Emerge**

#### **Pathway A: Direct Charity/Project Discovery** (Periodic)
**Actions:**
- Get matched with suggested charities based on history
- Search GlobalGiving API for specific projects
- Browse curated causes
- Make direct donations ($50-$500 range)

**Frequency:** Monthly or quarterly
**Purpose:** Significant impact on chosen causes

#### **Pathway B: Micro Matching** (Frequent)
**Actions:**
- See businesses supporting your charities
- Daily/weekly micro donations ($2-$50)
- Business matches multiply impact
- Build giving streaks

**Frequency:** 2-5 times per week
**Purpose:** Sustainable habit formation

---

### 3. **Comprehensive Impact Tracking**

#### **Donation Tracking** (Automatic)
- Forward ANY donation receipt
- Email scraping finds new receipts
- Both micro and direct donations tracked
- Score updates in real-time

#### **Volunteering Tracking** (Verified)
- Forward volunteer confirmation emails
- Manual upload with evidence (photos, certificates)
- Admin verification required
- Hours convert to score points
- Special volunteer badges earned

#### **Fundraising Tracking** (Verified)
- Track money raised for charities
- Submit campaign evidence (fundraising pages, receipts)
- Admin reviews and approves
- Multiplier effect on score
- Fundraiser badges and recognition

**Unified Impact Score:**
- Donations + Volunteering + Fundraising
- Complete philanthropic identity
- Tier progression accelerated
- Unlock exclusive perks

  - Connect with tier community

## 🔄 The Dual-Track Loops

### **Discovery Loop (Entry)**
```
Connect Email → AI Scans All Activity → Calculate Total Impact
(Donations + Volunteering + Fundraising) → Reveal Score & Tier 
→ Show Available Perks → Motivation to Level Up
```

### **Micro Matching Loop (Frequent)**
```
See Business Match → Quick $5-20 Donation → Instant 2-3x 
→ Update Score → Streak Progress → Daily Habit
```

### **Direct Giving Loop (Periodic)**
```
Monthly Check-in → Discover New Charity/Project → 
Make $50-500 Donation → Track Impact → Share Success
```

### **Social Impact Loop**
```
Make Impact → Score Updates → Public Profile Reflects Progress → 
Friends Discover → Social Validation → Competitive Motivation → 
More Engagement → Climb Leaderboard
```

### **Unified Progress Loop**
```
Donations + Volunteering + Fundraising Tracked → Score Grows → 
Tier Advances → Unlock Better Perks → Share Achievements → 
Inspire Others → Community Growth
```

## 🚀 The Do-Nation Difference

Do-Nation creates a comprehensive philanthropic identity that encompasses ALL forms of giving:

**1. Total Impact Discovery**: 
- Email AI reveals lifetime donations
- Verified volunteer hours added
- Fundraising achievements included
- Complete score across all activities

**2. Three Ways to Grow**:
- **Micro Matching** (Daily): $2-$50 frequent donations with business matches
- **Direct Giving** (Monthly): $50-$500 to discovered charities/projects
- **Volunteering & Fundraising** (Ongoing): Verified activities boost score

**3. Tiered Perks System**:
- **Bronze**: Entry perks, ethical discounts, 2x matches
- **Silver**: Event invites, 15% off brands, 2.5x matches
- **Gold**: VIP access, exclusive opportunities, 3x matches
- **Platinum**: Premium everything, mentor status, 4x matches

**4. Social Good Ecosystem**:
- Shop ethically with tier discounts
- Attend exclusive giving events
- Access higher match multipliers
- Connect with like-minded philanthropists

The platform transforms giving from a transaction into a lifestyle, where every action - donating, volunteering, or fundraising - contributes to your score and unlocks increasingly valuable perks that encourage continued engagement.
