Beta Tester Guide

Last updated: 26 March 2025

1) Getting Started

  • Create an account (Google/Microsoft sign‑in supported).
  • Read and accept the Beta Agreement, Terms, and Privacy Policy.
  • Verify your email if prompted.

2) Email Forwarding (Optional)

Forward donation receipts from Gmail/Outlook to enable automatic tracking. You can also use demo data to explore features.

3) Explore Key Features

  • Dashboard: Impact Score, category breakdown
  • Matching: Browse opportunities and preview matches
  • History: Donation records and receipts
  • Perks: Tier, benefits, events
  • Privacy: Control profile visibility

4) Known Beta Limitations

  • Features may change; bugs and UI rough edges expected
  • Processing time for forwarded emails may be a few minutes
  • Some integrations/social features are placeholders

5) Report Issues / Suggest Features

Use our Google Form to submit issues with steps to reproduce and expected vs actual behavior.

Open Bug Report Form

Severity quick reference:

  • S0: Critical outage or data loss
  • S1: Major breakage of core functionality
  • S2: Minor bug with workaround
  • S3: Enhancement/idea

6) Support

Email: joeheath@do-nation.space

7) Privacy & Data

  • Only emails you explicitly forward/authorize are processed.
  • Adjust visibility in Privacy Settings; request deletion via email.
  • Google API compliance: Google data only powers user‑facing features; we do not transfer it to third parties except for security, legal compliance, or with explicit consent.
